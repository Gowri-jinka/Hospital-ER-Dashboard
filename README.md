# 🏥 Hospital Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Hospital Patient Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze hospital patient data and provide meaningful insights into patient demographics, referrals, satisfaction levels, waiting times, and monthly patient trends.

This dashboard helps healthcare administrators and management teams monitor key patient-related metrics through interactive visualizations and data-driven reporting.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall patient statistics.
* Monitor patient waiting times.
* Evaluate patient satisfaction levels.
* Understand department referral patterns.
* Study patient demographic distribution.
* Track monthly patient trends.
* Enable interactive filtering and exploration of patient data.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides the following metrics:

* Total Patients: 9,216
* Average Patient Waiting Time: 35.26
* Average Patient Satisfaction Score: 1
* Total Departments: 8
* Total Case Managers: 480

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### Total Patients by Department Referral

Analyzes the number of patients referred to different hospital departments, helping identify departments with the highest patient volume.

### Patient Gender Distribution

Displays the distribution of patients based on gender using a donut chart.

### Total Patients by Month

Shows monthly patient counts to identify trends and fluctuations throughout the year.

### Patient Satisfaction Score by Department Referral

Evaluates patient satisfaction scores across different referral departments.

### Patient Details Table

Provides detailed patient-level information including:

* Full Name
* Department Referral
* Gender
* Race
* Age

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter dashboard data using:

* Full Name
* Patient Age
* Department Referral
* Month

These filters enable customized analysis and deeper exploration of patient records.
